About

A thesis project that turns spoken English into Slovak or Czech captions and speech.

What it is

Real-Time Speech Translation turns spoken English into Slovak or Czech captions and speech while the speaker is still talking. It began as a bachelor’s thesis at the Slovak University of Technology (STU FEI) and targets hybrid university meetings, where listeners need captions and translated speech from a single source.

Status: research prototype, not yet used in a live meeting.

Where it stands

AreaCurrent state
LanguagesEnglish to Slovak and English to Czech work. Slovak and Czech to English are experimental.
SpeedAbout 0.6–0.8 s from the end of a pause to the first translated sound on an M-series Mac. Listener delay per word is higher: a mean of 2.5 s on the same Mac in a dated benchmark.
SessionsOne session on one machine has been measured. Concurrent load has not.
VoiceThe default voice is generic Piper. Personal voice cloning is research.

The numbers and their limits are on the evidence section of the home page.

What it is built from

  • FastAPI and WebSocket for the server.
  • WebRTC VAD to cut speech into segments.
  • faster-whisper for speech to text.
  • Opus-MT through CTranslate2 for translation.
  • Piper for speech. OmniVoice is the voice-cloning research option and needs a GPU.
