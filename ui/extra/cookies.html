Cookies and local storage

Draft, written 2026-11-05 from the code. Not reviewed by a lawyer.

On this page

    No app cookiesThe app’s own code sets none.
    Local storageA few settings stay in your browser.
    Third parties mayGoogle sign-in and the YouTube player can set their own.

    What the app stores in your browser

    The app uses your browser’s local storage, not cookies. It is checked against the source code (no document.cookie or Set-Cookie use).

    KeyPurpose
    userTokenKeeps you signed in. Removed when you sign out.
    userName, userEmailShow who is signed in.
    userGuestMarks a guest session.
    themeRemembers light or dark mode.
    preferredAudioOutputRemembers your playback device.
    bp.playbackRateRemembers your translation speed setting.

    The app also keeps a service-worker cache of its own pages and scripts so the interface can load again on a flaky network. It never caches translations, voices or API responses.

    Third parties

    Fonts and icons come from this server. YouTube serves the demo thumbnail, and the video player loads from youtube-nocookie.com only when you press play; YouTube may set its own cookies at that point. The sign-in page loads Google’s sign-in script where Google login is configured, and Google may set cookies there. See the privacy policy.

    Clearing it

    Sign out to remove your token and profile keys. To remove everything, clear this site’s data in your browser settings.
