    What the software processes

    This describes what the software does. If you run it yourself, you are the operator and this applies to your installation.

    DataWhat happensStored?
    Microphone audioStreamed to the server you are connected to, converted to text and translated. Nothing is sent to a third-party speech service by default.No
    Guest sessionA guest gets a shared guest identity and a token that expires after 2 hours. No name, email or recordings are kept, and guests cannot record or store voices.No
    Account dataUsername, email address and a salted password hash (Argon2), only if you register. Guests have no account data.Yes, on the server
    Voice recordingsOnly if you record or upload a voice for a personal voice. You can rename or delete them in the voice menu.Yes, under your account
    Server logsTimings, language pairs and text lengths for troubleshooting. They contain no spoken words and no session tokens.On the server
    Session logsOff by default. If the operator enables them (BP_LOG_SESSIONS=1), the server writes transcripts, translations and timings to local files for accuracy review. Operators must tell participants before enabling this.Only if enabled
    Browser storageYour sign-in token, name and email, theme and chosen output device, kept in your browser. See cookies and local storage.In your browser

    Where your audio goes

    To the machine that runs the translator. If you run BP yourself (make run on localhost), nothing leaves your computer. On a shared server your audio goes to that server, where it is processed and not stored. The live page says which case you are in.

    Requests your browser makes to others

    Fonts and icons are served by this server. The demo thumbnail loads from YouTube, and the video player loads from YouTube only when you press play. YouTube sees your IP address then. If you use Google sign-in, Google processes that login and its sign-in script runs in your browser. There is no analytics or advertising code.

    Who is responsible

    For the author’s own instance the operator is Yegor Brusnyak (contact on the feedback page). If you run your own copy, you are the operator of that copy. Voice recordings are stored on the basis of your consent when you upload them, and you can withdraw it by deleting them.

    Sharing and retention

    The software does not share your data with anyone. Recordings and logs stay on the server until the operator deletes them. There is no automatic expiry yet, and account deletion is a manual request to the operator.

    Your rights

    You can ask the operator to access, correct or delete your data, or to restrict its processing. Contact the operator through the feedback page. You can also complain to the Slovak data protection authority (Úrad na ochranu osobných údajov). Voice recordings can reveal identity, so upload only recordings of your own voice.

    Changes

    This page is updated when the software’s data handling changes. The date above shows the last review.
