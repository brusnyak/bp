## Why lag exists (investigated)
Single speaker path on the Mac: ~0.4 s recogniser+hold, ~0.05 s MT, ~0.05 s voice. On the T4 the 1.97 s baseline comes from the Whisper agreement recogniser (two agreeing decodes), not from listeners. Room lag is the room's MT and voice competing for CPUs with the speaker's pipeline. Listeners of one language share one synthesis; languages are separate concurrent tasks.
