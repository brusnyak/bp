# BP Working Plan — Commercial Positioning & Phased Scope (2026-11-03)

**Purpose:** explicit, versioned working plan so nothing lives only in chat memory.
**Commercial positioning:** local, open-source, GDPR-friendly, zero per-minute cost, multi-language speech translation with optional own-voice cloning. Target: T4-class GPU (≤8 GB VRAM) or better; CPU-only baseline runs anywhere.

---

## 1. Commercial positioning

**Value proposition:** real-time speech translation that runs entirely on your hardware — no cloud, no per-minute fees, GDPR-friendly (audio never leaves the machine), multi-language via configurable packs, optional own-voice cloning.

**Competitors (paid cloud):**

| Service | Multi-language | Slovak | Czech | Pricing (2026) | Deployment |
|---------|---------------|--------|-------|----------------|------------|
| Microsoft Teams Interpreter | ~9 langs | ❌ | ❌ | ~$10/user/mo Premium | Cloud (Azure) |
| Google Meet Speech Translation | ~6 EN-anchored pairs | ❌ | ❌ | Workspace / Google AI plan | Cloud |
| KUDO AI Speech Translator | 77 langs | ✅ | ✅ | ~$0.80–1.25/min | Cloud widget |
| DeepL Voice-to-Voice | 40+ EU langs | ✅ likely | ✅ likely | ~$0.07–0.20/min | Cloud API |
| **BP local** | Multi-language via packs | ✅ (SK→EN experimental) | ✅ verified | Free (hardware + electricity) | Local / T4 or better |

**Local wins:** cost (free vs $0.07–1.25/min), privacy (GDPR-friendly, no audio leaves machine), no vendor lock-in.
**Local loses:** SK→EN quality (best open Slovak Whisper WER 0.2143 > 0.2 gate; real mic worse), no Slovak zero-shot voice cloning (XTTS/Chatterbox/Qwen3-TTS/Kokoro all exclude SK).

**Moat:** Slovak + Czech are NOT covered by Teams or Google Meet speech translation. Local EN↔{SK,CZ} that just works is a real gap the cloud players don't fill.
