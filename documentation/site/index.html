Live Speech Translation

Real-time speech translation that runs on your own machine: speak English, hear German, Slovak or Czech a moment later, with live captions. Bachelor's thesis project (STU FEI). Numbers on this page are generated from saved receipts in the repository and verified by make check-docs.

Verdict: one speaker, one direction runs live on a laptop (English → Slovak/Czech/German). Median lag 0.5-1.8 s on generated English on the M1 Pro Mac; the 2 s goal is met there but Slovak input under 2 s is not viable on this Mac — it needs a faster GPU (T4/L4/A10) or a streaming-native recogniser. German and Slovak are validated end to end through the real server on a Kaggle T4. Room mode is built and unit-tested; its real-model test is pending.

At a glance

What works today

EN→SK, EN→DE, EN→CS live on your own machine. Slovak output uses the kinit fine-tune (6.5% WER on native Slovak FLEURS; 19.8% on a non-native speaker's own reading). German validated end to end on a T4.

Goal

Median lag ≤ 2 s, voice starts mid-sentence, drift ≤ 0.5 s/min. Met on generated English on the Mac; not yet on real Slovak speech.

Main blocker

Slovak input under 2 s needs better compute. Whisper turbo is ~0.7 s/call on the Mac; Nemotron scores 28% on Slovak. Route: Nemotron 3.5 Slovak baseline on a T4 (~30 min), then fine-tune.

Voice

Piper is generic but fast (~0.15 s/piece). OmniVoice clones zero-shot (~0.66 s/piece) but is batch/QC only, not streaming. Owner's ear check pending to pick the route.
