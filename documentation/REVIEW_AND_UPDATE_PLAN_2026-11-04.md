## Decision and owner clarification

**The OpenCode summary is useful navigation but cannot be accepted as verification that the updates are properly set.** There is a real target-language regression, a browser/server authentication mismatch, unsupported device selection and incomplete route verification. Documentation cleanup and benchmark completion do not establish commercial or university readiness.

Best achievable outcome: an LHC operator can prepare an installed translation profile, confirm audio input/output with a sound check, run continuous translated captions and cloned speech, recover from faults and end all capture/playback reliably. Full-product acceptance includes voice cloning; generic Piper output is a diagnostic baseline and does not satisfy that complete specification.

Owner clarification in this chat: integration is **assumed** to be a layer beside existing LHC infrastructure; no further software/hardware details are available. Owner reports the Mac cannot deliver the planned cloning/full specification and considers better processing hardware necessary. This is an owner-reported constraint, not a fresh performance measurement. Preserve it as a hardware dependency. Do not silently reduce the product to generic TTS or pivot away from Slovak to declare success.

The virtual microphone provides one useful output boundary. It does not establish the room input path, return-audio capture, isolation from translated playback, listener routing, continuous capture, credentials, session lifetime or throughput. Start with an audio adapter hypothesis; defer any host-specific SDK integration until its interface is known. No AIS/Moodle/SSO requirement is established.

Files: `/Users/yegor/bp-benchmark-2026-11-04/confirm-canary-v1/results/canary-{sk,cs}-en-b5-full.json`. Their corpus chrF++ differs: `52.96609503815907` and `50.68767094809038`. State aggregation explicitly. Records say `acceptance=route_unverified`, with no human meaning grade.

The handoff's exact SK/CS/EN WER claims need a receipt/aggregation map. For these beam-5 files, micro WER is SK `0.05161290322580645`, CS `0.08819714656290532`, EN `0.04772599663110612`; mean WER is SK `0.04861612202966142`, CS `0.09297520152669762`, EN `0.046469268196179234`. They do not directly match the handoff's labels. Do not silently substitute one metric/run for another.

Do not convert “chrF++ >70” into a universal commercial gate. In the inspected handoff/README it lacks a task-specific human acceptance rationale. Set a bilingual human error/intelligibility rubric and use automatic scores as supporting evidence on a fixed dataset. Canary's GPU ASR/AST numbers are not the quality of the local Whisper→Opus→Piper route.

OmniVoice's current Kaggle ERROR status and Python-3.13 root-cause claim were not refreshed from Kaggle in this review. Treat them as historical handoff claims until the exact executed version and traceback are examined. The existing full-spec cloning requirement makes this a relevant bounded research track, but it does not supersede browser/auth/audio lifecycle repairs. RTF below 1 does not establish low first-audio latency or stable streaming, and short-phrase RTF above 0.4 is not automatically non-real-time.

Agree with **deferring MCP** absent a named integration consumer. Disagree that CLI/Python intrinsically provide every MCP capability or establish integration readiness. Reconsider only for an actual required host protocol; first prove the audio/session interface.

LHC context already exists in `documentation/PLAN_2026-11-03.md:101–118` and `reports/index-report.html`. Software, audio interfaces and embedding depth are explicitly unverified there. The new owner clarification supports an audio-layer assumption; it does not confirm an implemented university integration.
